Scenario A
Partial Absorption
Company absorbs X% of the tariff increase, passes (100−X)% to customers via price increases. Configurable slider 0–100%. Reduces but doesn't eliminate margin erosion. Used when customer price sensitivity is low.
Gross margin retained model
Scenario B
Full Pass-Through
Raises selling price to maintain current gross margin %. Includes 10% volume loss flag for products requiring >15% price increase, because elasticity matters. Maintains margin but risks customer churn.
Price-to-maintain formula
Scenario C
Supplier Switch
Redirects HIGH-risk products to an alternative country (Mexico, India, Vietnam, etc.). Includes the full transition cost model: PPAP qualification cost × n products + bridge inventory carrying cost for the qualification window.
PPAP + bridge inventory math
Most tariff tools stop
at "switch to Country X, save $Y."
The real decision is: if I switch from China to Mexico, my tariff drops from 145% to 25% - but my unit cost rises 15%, I need 90 days for PPAP re-qualification at $28,000 per part, and I need 3 months of bridge inventory to cover the qualification gap. What is my actual net saving, and when do I break even? That's the math procurement teams need. That's what TariffGuard computes.

Consultant Verdict
REAL PROBLEM The April 2025 tariff escalation is a live, documented crisis event, not a hypothetical scenario. Mid-market manufacturers are making these decisions right now.

SOUND FRAMEWORK The three-scenario model and PPAP + bridge inventory transition cost formula are the correct calculations for this procurement decision. This is what trade compliance consultants charge $50K to produce.

WATCH The gross saving in Scenario C is a proxy estimate, it doesn't fully account for the cost premium compounding with the new tariff rate. Tighten before a CFO review.

SYNTHETIC DATA Demo uses 10 fictional suppliers (SinoTech Ltd., CanSteel Corp., etc.) calibrated to realistic mid-market margins. Tariff rates are real USTR schedule. Frame as: "Synthetic demo portfolio calibrated to real tariff conditions, CSV upload supported for live data."
